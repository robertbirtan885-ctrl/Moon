# Privacy Policy — Moon · Period Calendar

**Last updated: 15 October 2026**

## In short

Moon collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and the app does not even request
internet permission. Everything you enter stays in your phone's memory.

## What data the app handles

The app lets you record:

- the name and age you enter when you first open it;
- period dates, flow intensity and spotting;
- symptoms, moods, cervical fluid, basal body temperature;
- sexual activity (protected, unprotected or other);
- pregnancy test results;
- notes you write yourself;
- app preferences: background, language, notification times, PIN code.

Some of this is sensitive data about health and sexual life. That is precisely why the app
is built so that it cannot send any of it anywhere.

## Where it is stored

In a single file, in the app's private storage, on your phone. Android does not allow
other apps to read that file.

The app **does not declare internet permission**, which means the operating system
technically forbids it any network connection. The data cannot be sent anywhere — not by
accident, and not through a future update, without that permission appearing visibly in
the app's permission list on Google Play.

The file is not encrypted separately by the app. It is protected by the app isolation
Android enforces and by your phone's own encryption. In addition, you can enable a PIN
code inside the app, which locks access to the screens.

## Who else has access

Nobody. There is no:

- user account or sign-in;
- developer server;
- usage analytics service;
- advertising network;
- third-party tracking module;
- data transfer to other companies.

## Permissions requested

The app requests three permissions, all of them for reminders:

- **Notifications** — for the reminders you choose yourself;
- **Start on device boot** — so reminders stay scheduled after your phone restarts;
- **Vibration** — for the notification alert.

It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

## Backups

**Automatic cloud backup is disabled.** The app explicitly tells Android not to include
your data in the Google backup. Nothing you record ends up in Google Drive.

**Transfer to a new phone** works: if you use the direct device-to-device transfer, the
data moves straight between the two phones without passing through the cloud. This applies
when the old phone runs Android 12 or newer; on Android 8–11 the system doesn't separate
transfer from cloud backup, so the app excludes both — there, use the manual export below.

**Manual export:** from *Settings → Your data* you can export a JSON file. From that moment
the file is in your care — if you email it or save it to a cloud service, that service's
privacy policy applies.

## Notifications

Reminders are generated locally by your phone, at the times you choose. Their content does
not pass through any external notification service.

## Deleting your data

- *Settings → Delete all data* removes every record.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything, including your profile and
  preferences.

We cannot delete your data for you, because we do not have it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from
them.

## Medical information in the app

The "Your body" section contains general reproductive health information for educational
purposes. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and cycle
predictions are statistical estimates based on your own data. The app is not a method of
contraception and does not replace a medical consultation. For any health concern, consult
a doctor.

## Your rights

Because the data never leaves your phone and never reaches us, the rights granted by the
GDPR — access, rectification, erasure, portability — are exercised directly in the app:
you see the data on screen, edit it, export it and delete it at any time, without asking us
for anything.

We do not process personal data as a controller, because we never receive your data.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated and the date at
the top of the document will reflect the change.
